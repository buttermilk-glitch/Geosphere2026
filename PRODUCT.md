# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

High school and undergraduate university students interested in geophysics, earth sciences, spatial technology, and geological exploration, along with event organizers and judges from ITS (Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember).

## Product Purpose

Serve as the official digital portal for Geosphere ITS 2026—a national geophysics competition hosted by Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember featuring 5 sub-competitions, event schedules, guidebooks, and registration workflows.

## Positioning

A high-impact, cinematic geophysics competition experience blending academic prestige with a theatrical dual-tone aesthetic ("Velvet Masquerade Royale").

## Operating Context

Visitors explore event sub-competitions, review schedules and guidelines, download rules/templates, and submit competition registrations across mobile and desktop devices.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Framework: Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4.
- Motion & Animation: Motion (Framer Motion), Lenis smooth scrolling, canvas animations.
- Code Style: Strict zero-comment policy in generated code files.

## Brand Commitments

- Event Name: Geosphere ITS 2026
- Dual-Tone Palette: Primary Canvas `#2C1E18` (Deep Espresso) & Secondary Spotlight `#FDFBF7` (Warm Cream)
- Visual Identity: Velvet Masquerade Royale (Depth, Mystery, Distinction)

## Evidence on Hand

- Project structure: Next.js 16 app router setup with Tailwind v4 in `app/globals.css`.
- Installed design skills: `impeccable` and `design-taste-frontend` in `.agents/skills/`.

## Product Principles

1. **Cinematic Craft:** Elevate the web browser into a dynamic, atmospheric stage with rich depth and fluid motion.
2. **Dual-Tone Distinction:** Maintain strict contrast and visual harmony between the `#2C1E18` canvas and `#FDFBF7` spotlight.
3. **Utility & Conversion:** Balance visual awe with intuitive navigation, leading visitors toward sub-competition discovery and registration.
4. **Production-Grade Quality:** Ensure high performance, clean responsive layouts, and anti-slop frontend standards.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain high contrast standards between text and background, support screen readers with semantic HTML5 elements, and enable full keyboard navigation.
